feldspar


noun
\ ˈfel(d)-ˌspär \

Definition of feldspar

: any of a group of crystalline minerals that consist of aluminum silicates with either potassium, sodium, calcium, or barium and that are an essential constituent of nearly all crystalline rocks

noun

Definition of wadi

1 : the bed or valley of a stream in regions of southwestern Asia and northern Africa that is usually dry except during the rainy season and that often forms an oasis : gully , wash
2 : a shallow usually sharply defined depression in a desert region
